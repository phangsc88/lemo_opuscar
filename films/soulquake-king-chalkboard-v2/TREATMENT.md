# SoulQuake — The crown we trust (Chalkboard remake)

90 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Original supplied Mandarin script and existing narrator retained. All picture staging is newly authored.

Three structures considered: (1) a chronological royal family tree; (2) a courtroom weighing three kings; (3) a pupil's crown-shaped shelter corrected by a teacher. Choose 3: the shelter makes the desire for security tangible, and the correction belongs naturally to chalkboard rather than a slide show.

Reference grammar: traditional classroom illustrated lectures (one diagram per idea), and Saul Bass's economy of symbolic silhouettes. Borrow economy and hierarchy, never specific compositions or designs.

Opening: a huge crown hangs over a small heart. A pupil writes an equals sign and a shield: the right person will protect us. Ending: the same panel now holds Christ, cross and empty tomb, with the heart still asking where to place its trust.

## Timed visual sequence
0–6: close crown/heart, slow pull to reveal the pupil's equation. Orange only marks kingship; pale yellow marks the pupil's hypothesis.
6–14: three small hand-drawn emblems (leader's flag, office case, paired hearts). A slow lateral move lets the viewer connect ordinary hopes to the crown.
14–16: lock wide while the physical front panel slides right.
16–23: back board: Israel's town and gathering, a crown overhead. Buildings and a shield make the request for protection concrete.
23–31: push to the crossed-out relationship with God, 1 Samuel 8:7 held as the narrator quotes it.
31–35: Saul's spear and crown, drawn at monumental scale.
35–38: pan right to David's sling and crown.
38–44: pull to see both converge on a cracked heart with 罪. Stop and allow the verdict to settle.
44–48: return to precisely the opening frame. An invisible felt pass wipes the false equation and royal shelter, retaining the heart. The silence carries the turn.
48–60: rewrite 应许 in the same area, underline the reference 2 Samuel 7:16, and draw a throne above a continuous covenant line.
60–71: push toward a large cross and an opened tomb. 基督 and 永恒的王 write themselves, with generous held reading time.
71–84: return to the heart; a white question mark asks the viewer where their trust belongs. A slow widening makes the relationship between heart and Christ visible.
84–90: room-wide summary, then a clean closer view of SOULQUAKE engraved in chalk on the rear board, held through the final line.
