# SoulQuake EP0 — Chalkboard remake

90-second Mandarin film, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, with Chinese primary labels and bilingual subtitles. A crown-and-shield hypothesis is replaced by the covenant promise, cross and empty tomb.

The picture and camera sequence are newly authored. Narration, source script, licensed chalk recordings and original sparse score are retained from the earlier version. The sound-effect mix is regenerated for the new animation.

Chinese lettering uses complete OFL LXGW WenKai glyphs, textured and progressively revealed. This is an intentional correction to malformed custom stroke drawings, requested by the user. These font reveals do not reproduce calligraphic stroke order.

Run `bash films/soulquake-king-chalkboard-v2/build.sh` from the repository. The existing repository Node/Python dependencies and Chromium are required. The export script copies the completed MP4 and SRT to `/workspace/shared/downloads/` under a V2 filename.
