# SoulQuake 0 — 谁才是真正的王？ | Stained Glass
90 seconds, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Same 27-clause Mandarin narration and bilingual subtitle timing as the approved Printed Long Take edition. A newly drawn stained-glass window supplies a different visual interpretation. The supplied Christian story expressly calls for the cross and resurrection, so those images take precedence over the library style's default secular-subject restriction. No God the Father or historical portrait is depicted.

## Three structures considered
1. A single central crown changes hands. Clear motif, but risks treating Christ as merely the next human king.
2. A gallery of separate windows. Easy chronology, but too many cuts and weak causal relationship between the failed thrones and the covenant.
3. **Chosen: a five-bay window with an upper promise rose.** Light first scans human hopes and Israel's royal demand, darkens over Saul and David, then descends from the promise rose into the cross and open tomb. The question returns to an ordinary figure on the floor as a projection. This gives God's promise an independent visual source and ends with the audience's response.

Logline: As a moving sunbeam exposes the fragility of human thrones, the enduring promise illuminates the empty tomb and asks where we place our trust.
Arc: desire for a savior → human kings fail → divine covenant → Christ's death and resurrection → personal question.

Benchmark: medieval Chartres windows for cobalt/ruby transmission and painted faces; monumental ecclesiastical architecture for scale. Learn craft, luminous colour and stone-space camera; copy no extant window layout, saints, specific poses, or music. No claim this is historical stained glass.
