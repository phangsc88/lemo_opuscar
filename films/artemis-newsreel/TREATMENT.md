# Artemis at Ephesus — 30-second 1920s Newsreel
User brief: new 30-second film about the Temple of Artemis in Ephesus, 1920×1080. Mandarin voice and Chinese subtitles inferred from preceding film. No storyboard approval requested for this new film.

## Three structures
1. Chronological fire/rebuild/excavation: good historical arc but risks implying invented footage of destruction.
2. Modern sightseeing tour: rejected because no verified current photographic sources available yet.
3. Print-led archaeological dispatch: selected. Real public-domain historical book illustrations and source pages, code-drawn newsreel packaging and schematic map. Old reconstructions clearly labelled; never generated ancient footage.

Logline: An energetic newsreel revisits an ancient wonder through historic print, its rebuilding and rediscovery.
Arc: Ephesus location / wonder → Ionic columns → destruction and rebuilding → nineteenth-century recovery → short held button.
Benchmark grammar: early sound newsreel ident/searchlights, spinning print, grease pencil and lively announcer; original title/emblem and synthesized score, no real broadcaster logo or melody.
