# 把时间用在光中 — final treatment

## Brief and adaptation
30 seconds, 1920×1080, 24fps, Mandarin narration, gentle music. User approved a six-panel warm parchment/loupe storyboard after changing from Brick Toy to Archive Light Table. The film adapts that style's continuous reading camera, loupe, glass reflections and annotation grammar to the user-supplied contemporary scripture-scroll graphic.

The original attachment was available as a visual reference but not as a local file. A blank parchment production plate was generated from the visual reference, and the supplied scripture wording was typeset programmatically for consistent accurate characters. The supplied graphic and its recreated composition are not presented as historic scans or museum objects. No external archives were needed.

## Structures considered and choice
Historical Ephesus tour: rejected because 30s would crowd out the pastoral application.
Three slogans: rejected because it lacked a coherent visual journey.
Single object, progressive reading: selected. One scroll and three loupe readings turn the question of busyness toward identity, wisdom, dependence on the Spirit, and prayer.
Benchmark grammar: precise documentary macro reading and physical glass-loupe optics; no copied demo layout, archive story, characters, melody or end card.

Logline: A closer reading of the scroll redirects attention from doing more to living wisely in response to Christ's redemption.
Arc: question → identity received → discernment → Spirit-enabled love → prayer.

## Picture and rhythm
One textured world and one continuous camera; smooth monotone keyframe interpolation. Optical magnification redraws the same source through a circular mask; fixed world anchors keep the chosen words centred. Bevel/reflection highlights and a soft contact shadow suggest the glass. A warm neutral source plate contrasts with a subtle cool light-table rim. Noto Serif CJK scripture, Noto Sans CJK subtitles. Modern commentary remains separate from the original scripture wording. The final image remains visible; no fade to black.

## Sound and cue map
Original restrained synthesized piano-like arpeggios and glass notes, 72BPM, nine 4/4 bars = 30 seconds. Foley: light starter clicks and three loupe-on-glass clacks at 4.95, 14.1, 19.15. Room/electrical bed. Music thins to near silence at 7.9–8.5 and 24.5–25.1. Local Kokoro zf_xiaoxiao Mandarin narration at 0.95 speed, using Misaki's v1.0-compatible Chinese frontend; no personal voice imitation. Mild voice compression and ducking. Final measured integrated loudness -13.9 LUFS, true peak -6.0dBTP.

## Validation and delivery

Six standalone and six final-mix narration segments passed independent SenseVoice ASR similarity threshold 0.92; standalone checks contain two near-homophone spelling substitutions, final mix one. This is not an exact-transcription claim. Visual review identified a cropped four-character lens target; lens diameter was enlarged and rechecked before the final render.
