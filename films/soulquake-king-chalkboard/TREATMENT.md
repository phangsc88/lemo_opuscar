# SoulQuake 0 — 谁才是真正的王？ | Chalkboard

90 seconds, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps. The approved Mandarin narration and bilingual subtitle timing are retained. The film takes place on a clean, deep-green sliding classroom blackboard. Chalk writes itself in true stroke order; no hand, chalk stick or eraser appears.

## Three structures considered

1. **A royal timeline across one long board.** Israel, Saul, David and Christ could read clearly from left to right, but it would turn the board into an infographic and weaken the style’s correction ritual.
2. **Three pupils’ answers to “Who is king?”** A pale-yellow second hand could offer leader, boss and partner before the teacher corrects it. This gives personality, but spends too much of the 90 seconds on repeated wrong answers.
3. **Chosen: one classroom equation corrected in place.** Open on `找到对的人 = 一切变好`, then expose Israel’s matching belief behind the sliding panel. Saul and David are written on opposite halves, but both arrows return to one heart marked `罪`. At the turn, one felt pass wipes `人的王 = 安全`; the same locked frame receives the red-orange answer `基督 = 永恒君王`. The correction turns the whole history into one lesson and uses the medium’s strongest native move.

Logline: A classroom lesson tests the hope that the right human king can fix everything, wipes that answer away, and rewrites Christ as the only eternal King worthy of trust.

Arc: modern hope → Israel asks for a king → Saul and David fail → the heart problem remains → God’s promise → Christ obeys, dies and rises → personal verdict.

## Benchmark

Learn the spare board economy of a strong mathematics lesson and the held-camera confidence of a master calligraphy demonstration: one claim, one correction, ample time to read. Copy no teacher’s handwriting, lesson layout, equation, music or classroom design. All board writing, diagrams and sound are original to this film.

## Chalk and room logic

- White chalk: structure, arrows, Scripture references and the heart problem.
- Red-orange chalk: the tracked `王`, God’s covenant line, the cross and the final answer.
- Pale yellow chalk: what people hope a human leader will provide.
- Deep ink-green slate, medium walnut frame, chalk tray, two sliding panels, warm plaster wall and one school clock. The board begins freshly wiped with no ghost lessons.
- Chinese board words are authored as centre-line strokes and rendered through the chalk engine. English uses the engine’s single-line handwriting font. The camera always faces the slate flat.

## Native move

At 43–47 seconds the camera does not move. One smooth felt path wipes the two human crowns and the equation `人的王 = 安全`, but it passes around the heart marked `罪`. In the same physical space, orange chalk begins the covenant promise and later writes `基督 = 永恒君王`. The before and after share the same framing, so the theological correction is visible rather than merely narrated.

The orange `王` is the tracked component. It retains its colour and height in Israel’s request, Saul, David and the final `永恒君王`; only its context changes. Orange is otherwise reserved for the promise, cross and final answer.
