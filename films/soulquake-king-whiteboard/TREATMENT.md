# SoulQuake 0 — 谁才是真正的王？ | Whiteboard Explainer

90 seconds, landscape 1920×1080, 24 fps. The approved Mandarin narration and bilingual subtitle timing are retained. The film takes place on one physical glossy dry-erase board. All words and diagrams are written stroke by stroke; floating markers, magnets and an eraser move without hands.

## Three structures considered

1. **A vertical sermon outline.** Easy to follow, but it would feel like slides placed on a board rather than a spatial explanation.
2. **A left-to-right biblical timeline.** Strong chronology, but it underplays the opening and ending question about present-day trust.
3. **Chosen: a question-map shaped like a crown.** The film begins inside a huge question mark, travels through six connected regions, and pulls back to reveal that the route forms a crown outline. The human crown magnet fails at the centre; the red covenant line completes the missing crown point through the cross and empty tomb. The final camera returns to the opening question, now answered.

Logline: A marker-led journey across one board tests the people and powers we trust, erases the false solution, and connects God's promise to the eternal King.

Arc: present hopes → Israel's demand → Saul and David → the heart problem → covenant promise → Christ's obedience, death and resurrection → personal trust.

## Native move

At 38–44 seconds, the felt eraser rewinds the blue chain from David back through Saul and the requested king. It removes the drawings to about 90%, leaving pale blue ghosts, but the black `罪` inside the heart remains. This makes the theological turn visible: replacing one human king with another does not solve the heart. The next red stroke begins from that unremoved heart and travels to the covenant promise.

The blue crown magnet is the only movable hero prop. It drops at the opening, slides to Israel, moves from Saul to David, then is lifted away. The red crown magnet appears only after the resurrection diagram.

## Drawing system

Warm white glossy board, faint old-lesson ghosts, dry-marker speckle, micro-scratches and a slow diagonal window sheen. Three semantic colours:

- dark charcoal: structure, Scripture words, heart problem and questions;
- blue: human solutions, requested kings and worldly security;
- red: covenant promise, Christ and the trustworthy answer.

Every line uses slow-fast-slow stroke progress, pooled starts, slight chisel variation, overshot corners and incomplete closures. Marker shadows grow during hops; pens park outside the frame between jobs. No hands, cursors, block-text animation or slide cuts.

## Titles and subtitles

All board text is written in the retained CJK face with hand wobble and stroke-order approximation. Board labels are 44–100 px; the question and final answer are 130–180 px. Burned subtitles sit in an off-white rounded label at the bottom, Chinese above English, with a short red marker dash. Clause timing follows `timeline.json`.

## Sound and cue map

Original 72 BPM clockwork-minimal cue: pizzicato low strings, marimba-like wooden tones, woodblock and sparse glockenspiel-like signals. No generic piano. Each visible stroke has felt-on-gloss hiss and a small nib tick; blue and red markers have slightly different bands. Crown magnets have steel clicks and board thumps; the eraser has modulated felt rub. Music and foley duck under narration.

- 0–14: lone wood pulse, question-mark squeak, crown magnet clack.
- 14–31: blue-marker pulse adds pizzicato; hold and thin under 1 Samuel 8:7.
- 31–44: two phased figures for Saul and David, then both stop under the eraser rewind.
- 44–60: near-silence, red nib tick, rising one-note-per-stroke covenant figure.
- 60–74: the two figures align in unison at `复活`; one clear metal ping on the red crown.
- 74–86: second near-silence beneath the personal question.
- 86–90: open fifth, marker cap, soft room tone.

Target −14 LUFS and true peak below −1.5 dBTP.
