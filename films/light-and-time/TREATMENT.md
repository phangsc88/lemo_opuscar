# 把时间用在光中 — 30-second treatment / storyboard v1
User brief: Brick Toy, Mandarin, gentle music, 1920x1080 landscape, storyboard approval before production.
This is a condensed adaptation, not a reading of the full sermon. No external supplied assets. Default warm Mandarin storyteller, original gentle toy-piano/plucked score. No voice generated before approval.

## Structures considered
1. Historical journey: paper tower → Ephesus → present. Rejected for 30s: history would crowd out grace and response.
2. Three slogans on brick signs. Rejected: readable, but little story or emotional turn.
3. One builder, one choice: distracted tower-building → Christ's rescue/new identity → redirected time expressed in love. Selected: concrete opening and ending; grace precedes action.

Logline: A distracted brick builder discovers that life is not about collecting more achievements; belonging to Christ changes what they build with their time.
Arc: busyness → receive new identity → choose wisely → love and prayer.
Benchmark: tabletop macro toy photography (material, scale, focus); tactile stop-motion shorts (held poses, clicks). No copied shots, characters, brands, tunes. Characters built from ordinary blocks, not trademark minifigure shapes.

## Shot / voice / cue map
72 BPM, gentle 4/4, nine bars = 30s. Cuts intentionally span unequal phrase lengths; section times below are provisional until Mandarin voice timing.
1 / 0–4s / macro push: unfinished paper tower on real wood; rigid brick character reaches toward scattered trophy bricks and clock. VO: 纸塔游戏提醒我们：忙碌，不等于做好重要的事。
2 / 4–8s / overhead sweep: 24 clock tiles and competing achievement tokens encircle tiny unfinished tower; character pauses. VO: 谁来决定，时间该花在哪里？
3 / 8–14s / low close, rack focus: simple brick cross, empty tomb with rolled stone; builder stands in light, open hands. VO: 基督已经救赎我们，使我们成为光明的子女。
4 / 14–19s / tracking hands: same primary bricks click into a sturdy foundation; builder turns from trophy toward open-book tile. VO: 所以，要明白主的旨意，爱惜光阴。
5 / 19–25s / intimate medium: builder offers a brick to a second builder; together they build a bridge. VO: 靠着圣灵，学习爱神、爱人。
6 / 25–30s / slow high pullback: finished small bridge, tower and family on real desk; clock and blank prayer notebook behind. VO: 主啊，帮助我，把时间用在你所喜悦的事上。
Total narration 88 Chinese characters excluding punctuation; estimate ~30s, confirm recorded timing after approval. Historical Ephesus exposition and 2036 prompt omitted for clarity; no implication salvation is earned through productivity.

## Sound design
0–4: quiet room, paper rustle, light toy-piano motif, clock click.
4–8: clock ticks; music thins to near silence at the question.
8–14: warm glockenspiel resolves, single brick click; no voice of God, no personification of Holy Spirit.
14–19: plucked acoustic motif, foundation clicks on grid.
19–25: gentle rising phrase, tiny footsteps and shared brick click.
25–30: music falls back before prayer, sustained final note and room tone.

## Visual / subtitle design
Real textured wooden desk, muted paper/ceramic props, saturated blue/red/yellow ABS bricks, rounded beveled bodies, lip studs, clearcoat, AO contact seams, neutral daylight softboxes, physical DOF, 2x supersampling. Characters step on twos at 12fps; camera on ones at 24fps. The bridge assembly is the signature medium-native move. No collapse animation required.
Friendly Chinese font, white on dark rounded toy-like subtitle pill. Storyboard includes full VO below each frame, not burned into preview image. Final subtitles split by spoken clauses, hold >=1.8s with tail; final prayer card >=4s.
